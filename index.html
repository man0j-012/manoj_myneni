Who Am I?

My name is Manoj Dattatreya Myneni. My professional background as a software engineer spans more than 3 years, during which I have worked across distributed systems, robotics software, full-stack development, iOS development, and applied machine learning. I completed my Master’s in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago, where I focused on systems programming and networking.

I enjoy developing and troubleshooting real systems, whether it is a distributed backend service, ROS System, a production web or iOS feature, or an ML-driven component that needs to run reliably at scale. My experience in research labs, industry roles, and personal engineering projects taught me how systems behave in practice, not just in theory.

Over the past few months, I have been giving my full effort to grow as an engineer, spending long hours learning and building systems. I am highly motivated to bring that same energy, and I am open to roles in systems engineering, robotics software, distributed infrastructure, full stack development, and other areas where this range of experience is valuable.
